    Go-to-market — portfolio lanes

    • Lane 1 — core diploid hybrid: Crimson/Charleston-type fruit, 9–12 kg target, rind toughness, 10+ °Brix, heat/drought set and foliar disease tolerance.
    • Lane 2 — premium seedless: mini/medium triploid plus pollinizer sold as a protocol package for Caracas, Valencia and Maracaibo retail channels.
    • Lane 3 — risk-management package: rootstock/grafted option for repeated cucurbit soils, nematode/Fusarium/MNSV-watchlist fields and high-value coastal blocks.
    • Lane 4 — OP trust-builder: low-cost treated OP packs for smallholder channels where saved seed dominates.

    Trial priorities & success metrics

    From the report’s execution playbook.

    1. Guárico dry-season irrigated heat-set trial — stress flowering, set and rind quality.
    2. Anzoátegui central-pivot / eastern plains trial — benchmark against Charleston Gray / Crimson-style references.
    3. Falcón/Paraguaná pack-out trial — only with an active packhouse or distributor partner.
    4. Lara/Portuguesa drip/fertigation trial — Brix, uniformity and root vigor.

    Commercial success metrics

    • Marketable yield (t/ha) and pack-out %
    • °Brix and flesh quality; hollow heart / cracking / sunburn incidence
    • Emergence and stand uniformity; disease incidence through harvest cycles

    Strategic conclusion

    The Venezuela watermelon seed market is commercially real but execution-sensitive. A realistic FY2026–FY2027 entrant should underwrite a ~$3.1M TAM, ~$2.16M SAM and ~$129k near-term SOM rather than an export-led upside story. Enter with local trials, clean seed, heat/drought and rind-quality claims, and a distributor/nursery playbook — not reliance on formal trade data alone.
