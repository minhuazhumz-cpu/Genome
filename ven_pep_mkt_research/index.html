    Segment area split

    Because Venezuela does not publish accessible recent pepper acreage by type, I split the 10,126 ha national base into commercial typology buckets using local agronomy evidence, culinary demand, and production-system logic:

    Segment Area share Area (ha) Why this share
    Ají dulce open-field / semi-commercial C. chinense 35% 3,544 Ají dulce is culturally central in Venezuelan cuisine and widely referenced locally; Rosita, Pepón, Llanero/Llanerón and Oriental types anchor domestic demand.
    Open-field pimentón / sweet bell / blocky pepper 30% 3,038 Pimentón/sweet pepper is a major commercial fresh-market segment and likely the main standardized commercial pepper bucket.
    Hot/chili and specialty fresh Capsicum 15% 1,519 Smaller but relevant specialty/hot pepper channel; includes non-ají-dulce hot and condiment peppers.
    Protected / greenhouse pimentón and specialty peppers 10% 1,013 Small area, high seed value; justified by Miranda/Altos Mirandinos greenhouse evidence and protected pepper disease outbreaks.
    Local OP/saved-seed / low-input smallholder channel 10% 1,013 Important food-security and informal channel but low premium seed monetization.
